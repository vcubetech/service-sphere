# Building Permits and Ground Coverage (SAMPLE POLICY)

> Sample knowledge-base document for the RAG demo. Replace it with your municipal
> authority's actual building bye-laws / zoning regulations before production use.

## Permit requirement

No person shall erect, re-erect or make material alterations to any building without a
building licence and a sanctioned plan from the local planning authority. Construction
that starts without a sanctioned plan is treated as unauthorised construction.

## Ground coverage limits

Maximum ground coverage is the percentage of the plot area that may be covered by the
building footprint, as stated on the sanctioned plan. Indicative limits:

- Residential plots up to 360 m²: 65% ground coverage
- Residential plots above 360 m²: 50% ground coverage
- Commercial plots: 50% ground coverage
- Industrial plots: 45% ground coverage

Where the sanctioned plan specifies a lower ground coverage, the sanctioned value applies.

## Deviation from the sanctioned plan

Deviation of up to 5% of the setback or coverage may be regularised by paying a
compounding fee. Deviations beyond 5% must be removed by the owner. The authority issues
a deviation notice giving the owner 15 days to respond before further action.

## Unauthorised construction procedure

1. Field inspection by the assistant engineer, with photographs and measurements.
2. Provisional order and stop-work notice to the owner / builder.
3. Hearing: the owner may produce the licence and sanctioned plan within 7 days.
4. Confirmation order; unauthorised portions may be sealed or demolished at the owner's cost.

Remote-sensing evidence (satellite or drone imagery) can trigger an inspection but must be
verified on the ground before any punitive order is issued.

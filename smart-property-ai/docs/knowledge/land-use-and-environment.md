## Agricultural land conversion

Agricultural land may not be used for non-agricultural purposes (housing, commercial,
industrial) without a land-use conversion order (NA order) from the Deputy Commissioner.
Construction on agricultural land without conversion attracts a penalty and the
unauthorised use may be ordered to stop.

## Tree felling and green cover

Felling of trees on private or public land requires prior permission from the Tree
Officer. For each tree felled, compensatory plantation of at least two trees within the
same ward is normally required. Residential plots above 240 m² should retain at least
one tree per 240 m² of plot area.

## Construction completion period

A building licence is valid for two years from the date of issue for completion of the
work and may be renewed once for one year on application. If no work is carried out for
six months, the site must be secured (fencing, covered excavations) to prevent accidents.
Stalled sites with open excavations are a public-safety risk and should be inspected.

## Demolition

Demolition of a building requires prior intimation to the authority. Debris must be
disposed of only at designated construction-and-demolition waste sites. Demolition of
listed heritage structures is prohibited without heritage committee approval.

# Land Survey Records and Encroachment (SAMPLE POLICY)

> Sample knowledge-base document for the RAG demo. Replace it with your state's actual
> land-revenue / survey manual before production use.

## Survey numbers

Every land parcel is identified by a survey number and, where the parcel has been
partitioned, a sub-division number (for example Sy. No. 123/4). The boundary recorded
in the survey sketch (FMB / tippan) is the legal boundary for that parcel.

## Encroachment on private land

When a structure built on one survey number extends onto an adjoining survey number:

1. Either owner may apply to the Tahsildar for a boundary re-survey (hadbast).
2. The licensed surveyor fixes the boundary in the presence of both parties.
3. If an encroachment is confirmed, the encroacher is given notice to remove it.
   Disputes about title are referred to the civil court.

## Encroachment on government land

Any structure on government land (gomala, roads, tank beds, canal margins) is an
encroachment. The Tahsildar may issue an eviction notice and remove the encroachment
after a hearing. Repeat offences can be prosecuted under the land-revenue law.
Government-land encroachments should be treated as high priority.

## Use of satellite imagery

Satellite imagery is geo-referenced to an accuracy of a few metres, so a boundary
crossing smaller than the imagery accuracy should be confirmed by a ground survey
before notices are issued.
